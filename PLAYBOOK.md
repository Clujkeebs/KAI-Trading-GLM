# PLAYBOOK.md — the operator's method

Loaded into the trading model's prompt alongside `SOUL.md`. This is the operator's own
framework, condensed to the parts a machine can act on. The full document is in
[docs/the-crypto-playbook.md](docs/the-crypto-playbook.md). Where this and the charter
disagree, the charter wins.

## Sectors move, not coins

Money rotates between narratives. When one name in a sector runs hard, the trade is the
laggard in that same sector, not the name that already moved. Chasing the leader after the
move is paying for someone else's entry.

## The three categories

Sort every candidate before anything else:

1. **Has not run yet — buyable.** Depressed price, sector with momentum, improving
   fundamentals or volume.
2. **Ran and broke — avoid.** Far below its long-term high with nothing recent to change
   the story. A low price is not a cheap price; the market moved on. Needs a specific,
   recent catalyst to be worth anything.
3. **Running hard right now — hold or trim, rarely buy.** A big green day in a strong
   sector usually continues. Trim into parabolic moves, do not sell into confirmation.

## Buying

Take a position when the case is affirmative, not merely tolerable:

- The pair is moving with the market or better. Flat while everything rallies is a red flag,
  not a discount.
- Upside is worth the risk — roughly 2:1 or better against a stop you would actually honour.
- There is a reason it should move: a catalyst, a rotation into its sector, volume arriving.
- Size by conviction. The strongest case gets the most capital; speculative names get least.

Absent a reason it should move, it is not a trade, it is a hope. Say so and pass.

## Selling

- **Dead money.** Flat or red while the market rallies means the capital is trapped. Free it.
- **Broken names.** Far below the long-term high, no catalyst, no development — take the loss.
- **Overextension.** After a large run with a pullback in view, trim half. Half locks the gain
  and keeps the exposure; all-or-nothing is a coin flip.
- **Speculative positions.** Honour the stop immediately. Small losses are tuition.
- **Winners on a green day.** Not a sell. That is the thesis working.

## Positioning

- Spread across sectors, not across names — ten names in one narrative is one bet.
- Hold a foundation position in something durable; growth comes from the smaller sleeves.
- Never fully invested. Cash is optionality, and the account should never be forced to sell
  at a bad price to act.
- Churn is the beginner's tax. Doing nothing is frequently the correct cycle.

## Evidence

Treat prices, ranges and news as a spread of opinion, not fact: where a name sits inside the
range of expectations matters more than any single target. A tight range is confidence, a wide
one is nobody knowing. Never assert a level, a fill or a catalyst that is not in front of you.
